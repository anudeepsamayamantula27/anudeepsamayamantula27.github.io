SRH draws a top-four audience but is valued $76M behind CSK. Some of that is history (5 titles against 1). Some may be fixable.

02 / 08 · My hypothesis

Fans have nothing to follow between seasons.

The Sunrisers group also plays in SA20 (Eastern Cape) and The Hundred (Leeds). I assumed Hyderabad fans didn't know, or didn't care. So I tested it.

03 / 08 · How I tested it

339 fans in 3 days.

  • 339real responses
  • 246SRH fans
  • 141living in Hyderabad

A 3-minute survey I designed, shared by SRH fan accounts on X and by r/SunrisersHyderabad. About 20,000 bot entries were filtered out.

04 / 08 · What I found

I was wrong.

  • 98%of SRH fans already knew about Eastern Cape and Leeds
  • 67%follow them regularly

Awareness isn't the gap. These fans already follow all three teams, alone, on their phones.

05 / 08 · The real gap

Loyal fans. Little revenue.

  • 37%have never watched SRH at the home stadium; getting tickets is the top complaint
  • 75%own no official SRH merch (43% none, 32% only copies)

The gap is turning that loyalty into tickets, merch and memberships SRH can count.

06 / 08 · The demand

They'd show up. Some would pay.

  • 61%of Hyderabad fans rate a local watch-along for Eastern Cape or Leeds 4–5 out of 5
  • 54%would pay for a yearly fan membership, mostly up to ₹500

People say yes more easily than they turn up, so the pilot has to prove it.

07 / 08 · My solution

One Orange Army: 3 months → 7.

Today: Hyderabad gathers for 3 months a year

Watch-along Nights in Hyderabad for Eastern Cape and Leeds matches, and a Passport whose stamps are redeemed at the IPL opener.
