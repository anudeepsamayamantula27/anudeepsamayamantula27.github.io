The big idea · a concept

One Orange Army,
three seasons.

The Orange Army has one season. The Sunrisers group has three. My idea: bring Eastern Cape and Leeds home to Hyderabad, so the fans who live for the IPL follow all three teams, all year.

JAN – FEB · SA20Sunrisers Eastern Cape3 titles in 4 seasons. Season 5 runs 17 Jan – 21 Feb 2027.
MAR – MAY · IPLSunrisers HyderabadThe window Hyderabad's fan calendar is built around.
JUL – AUG · THE HUNDREDSunrisers LeedsMen's games land at about 7–11 pm in India.
01Orange Army NightsWatch-alongs for Eastern Cape and Leeds matches at partner venues and campuses in Hyderabad, run like a home game.
02Second screenShort local-creator reels that introduce "your other Sunrisers", plus a live watch-along stream for fans at home.
03Orange Army PassportA card stamped at every Night. Stamps carry into the IPL season, so following all three teams earns something at home.

Pilot it in SA20 2027, before the IPL: 2 venues and 1–2 campuses, 4 Nights on Eastern Cape match days, Passport redeemed at the IPL opener. Estimated cost: about ₹22K–78K plus a host.
